# No Trial System - User Guide

## 🎯 What Changed?

Blumebyte has removed the free trial system. Companies now operate on a **pay-first** model.

---

## 🆕 New Company Registration Flow

### Step 1: Create Company Account
1. Go to `/company-signup`
2. Fill in company details:
   - Company Name
   - Company Size
   - Industry
3. Create admin account:
   - Admin Name
   - Email
   - Password
4. Click **"Create Company Account"**

✅ **Account Created!**

### Step 2: Check Account Status

After registration, you'll have:
- ✅ Company account created
- ✅ SuperAdmin login credentials
- ✅ Access to dashboard
- ⚠️ **0 licenses** (cannot add employees yet)
- ⚠️ **Subscription: Inactive**

### Step 3: Purchase Licenses

To start adding employees:

1. **Login** with your admin credentials
2. **Navigate** to Subscription page (from main menu)
3. **Choose** a plan:
   - **Monthly**: $6/employee/month
   - **Yearly**: $5/employee/month (17% savings)
   - **Custom**: Contact sales
4. **Select** number of licenses you need
5. **Pay** via Paystack
6. **Done!** Licenses activated immediately

### Step 4: Add Employees

After purchasing licenses:
1. Go to Employee Management
2. Click "Add Employee"
3. Fill in employee details
4. Employee account created ✅

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can I test the system before paying?

**A:** Yes! You can create a company account for free and explore the dashboard. However, you'll need to purchase licenses to add employees and use the full system.

### Q: What happens if I don't purchase licenses?

**A:** Your company account remains active, but you cannot:
- Add employees
- Use employee features
- Access employee portal
- Generate payslips
- Track attendance

You can purchase licenses anytime to activate these features.

### Q: Can I start with just 1 license?

**A:** Yes! There's no minimum. Purchase exactly the number of licenses you need.

### Q: What if I need more licenses later?

**A:** Easy! Just go to the Subscription page and upgrade. You can:
- Add more licenses to your current plan
- Switch from Monthly to Yearly
- Upgrade to a custom plan

### Q: Do licenses expire?

**A:** 
- **Monthly**: Renews automatically each month
- **Yearly**: Renews automatically each year
- You can cancel anytime

### Q: What happens if my payment fails?

**A:** 
- You'll receive a notification
- Grace period of 7 days
- After grace period, employees are deactivated
- Reactivate by updating payment method

### Q: Can I get a refund if I don't use all licenses?

**A:** Contact our support team. We handle refunds on a case-by-case basis.

### Q: Is there a long-term contract?

**A:** No! Both monthly and yearly plans are flexible:
- Cancel anytime
- No cancellation fees
- Export your data before leaving
